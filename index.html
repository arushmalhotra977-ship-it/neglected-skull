What is bioinformatics?

Bioinformatics combines biology, computer science and data analysis to help us understand biological information.

🧬

DNA: life's instruction book

DNA stores biological instructions. A DNA sequence is written using four letters: A, T, G and C.

ATGC
💻

Computers find patterns

DNA sequences can be very long. Software helps researchers compare sequences, organize data and look for patterns efficiently.

🔬

Where is it used?

Bioinformatics is used in genetics research, evolution studies, biodiversity, agriculture and infectious-disease research.

Quick questions

Learn it. Explain it.

Useful points to remember for a school science exhibition.

What does DNA stand for?

DNA stands for deoxyribonucleic acid. It carries genetic information in most living organisms.

What do A, T, G and C mean?

They stand for adenine, thymine, guanine and cytosine. In the usual DNA double helix, A pairs with T and G pairs with C.

Can this app identify a person or diagnose a disease?

No. This is a classroom demonstration using short invented sequences. It does not identify anyone, interpret real genetic samples or diagnose medical conditions.

Is this a complete bioinformatics tool?

No. It demonstrates a basic sequence-comparison idea. Real bioinformatics may involve much larger datasets, specialized algorithms, scientific validation and privacy protections.

What is the main takeaway?

Bioinformatics uses computers to help analyze biological information. This app shows how a simple program can compare data and display a clear result.
